(01) Who we are

A supplier and trader of disposable tableware.

K K Marketing is a supplier and trading company based in Margao, Goa. We deal in disposable products — paper plates, bowls, cups, areca leaf plates and the trays and cutlery that go with them.

Customers buy from us in bulk for the best rates, or pick up just what they need from the shop at Pajifond. Either way, you deal directly with the people who run it.
